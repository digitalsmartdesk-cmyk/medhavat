Why it's different

What stone milling means for a ready mix.

"The slow grinding of rice and lentils on stone preserves the texture that makes an idli truly soft — steel blades and heat strip that away."

Most packaged idli mixes are made from pre-cooked, high-heat-processed rice flour, which is why they often turn out dense or gummy. Our Idli Mix is stone-milled from parboiled rice & urad dal at low speed, keeping the starches intact and the texture light.

Just mix with water, rest for 10–15 minutes, and steam. You get soft, spongy idlis with the same authentic South Indian taste — without the overnight soaking and grinding your grandmother had to do.
