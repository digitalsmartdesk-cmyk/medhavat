Why Sharbati Wheat?

"Sourced from the fertile plains of Madhya Pradesh, Sharbati wheat is prized for its golden colour, natural sweetness, and high gluten content that makes rotis incredibly soft."

Stone-ground using traditional chakki methods at low speed, our Whole Wheat Atta preserves the natural oils, bran and germ that high-speed roller mills routinely strip away. The result is flour that smells of fresh wheat — and rotis that stay soft for hours.

We source Sharbati wheat directly from farms in Madhya Pradesh, where the black soil and climate produce grains with a distinctive golden hue and naturally sweet taste. Every batch is milled fresh in small quantities so nothing sits on a shelf losing its goodness.
